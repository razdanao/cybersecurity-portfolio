## Review notes

- `Class 5 Participation.pdf` has a title mismatch with its filename.
- The Class Presentation 6 slide gives a dynamic port range starting at `46,152`; the conventional IANA range starts at `49,152`.
- Course examples are learning materials, not automatically verified final code. Review them before reuse.
- Screenshot filenames overlap between the supplied batches, so this index refers to screenshots by visible heading rather than number.
